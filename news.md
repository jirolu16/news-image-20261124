## Lead: The Boss Is Back

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday
23 November, restoring the company's founder to the top job. President Trump
says he personally persuaded the board to make the change. Democrats are
calling for an investigation into the White House's role in the decision.
Nvidia's shares jumped about 30 percent on the news, and Wall Street
recovered roughly half of the previous week's losses, although AI stocks
remained volatile.

Reported by: CWEB Daily Shocker, 24 November; JAWS, Global and U.S.
Strategic News Brief, 24 November.

## Climate: COP31 ends with no deal

The COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, ended on Sunday 22 November
without an agreement, the first United Nations climate summit to close with
no deal since Copenhagen in 2009. The talks ran two days into overtime,
making this the longest COP on record. The European Union walked out after
the presidency's final text made no mention of fossil fuels. The rest of the
package fell with the main text, including a target for electricity to meet
35 percent of final energy demand by 2035 and the planned adaptation funding
for vulnerable countries. The unfinished work moves to the intersessional
talks in Bonn, and the next summit, COP32, is due in Addis Ababa.

Reported by: EU Insider; ENR; Beyond 2C, drawing on Carbon Brief; OilPrice;
Reuters Sustainable Switch.

## Strait of Hormuz: tankers hit mines

Iran says three oil tankers hit mines and caught fire in the Strait of
Hormuz. Tehran is refusing to negotiate until its seven conditions are met,
and says it will use all the tools it has to confront the United States
naval blockade, which was reinstated on 14 July.

Reported by: Roman Report (Middle East Forum); JAWS news brief; Just
Security, Early Edition, 24 November.

## Ukraine: strike on Zaporizhzhia

An overnight Russian attack on the city of Zaporizhzhia killed two people
and wounded about 40, including four children, according to the regional
governor, Mykhailo Semikin. The attack struck an apartment building.

Reported by: Just Security, Early Edition, 24 November (Associated Press);
CWEB Daily Shocker.

## Gaza: Hamas security chief killed

Israeli airstrikes in Gaza killed Naeem Abu Nahel, the director of Hamas's
internal security apparatus, and at least six other people, including two
police officers, according to medics and police.

Reported by: Just Security, Early Edition, 24 November; Reuters, via The
Global Eye.

## Indonesia: MSCI denies any promise to Prabowo

Index provider MSCI denied on Tuesday that it had assured President Prabowo
Subianto it would not downgrade Indonesia. Its consultation on reclassifying
Indonesia to a Standalone Market remains open until 30 April 2027, with a
decision due by 30 June 2027. The denial followed Bloomberg's report of a
10 November call between Prabowo and MSCI's chief executive, Henry
Fernandez, a call arranged by Ray Dalio. Jakarta's benchmark IHSG rose
regardless: it opened up 1.29 percent at 5,283 and closed up 0.49 percent at
5,241.59.

Reported by: KabarBursa; AmCham Update; Bloomberg Technoz; RCTI+.
